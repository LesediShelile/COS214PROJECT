Detailed Description

Composite of the Composite pattern: a WorkItem that owns child WorkItems (Tasks or other Stages).

Abstract: SequentialStage and ParallelStage decide in which order the children become available (activateChildren()).

Design decision: assign/start/complete/reject act on ONE unit of work, so a Stage refuses them with std::logic_error. Act on its Tasks instead. makeAvailable() starts the stage, cancel() cancels every unfinished child, and isFinished()/isAvailable() aggregate over the children.
