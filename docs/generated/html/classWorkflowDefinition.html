Detailed Description

Template blueprint describing the structure and behavior of a workflow process.

Acts as the Product of the Builder pattern and the client of the Prototype pattern. Owns the root of a WorkItem tree and the head of an ApprovalHandler chain. Once frozen, its internal structure becomes immutable, ensuring that any WorkflowInstance created from it receives an exact, isolated clone of its tree. A definition never executes directly: its tasks are never assigned or completed.

Note
Instances cannot be generated until the definition is frozen.
